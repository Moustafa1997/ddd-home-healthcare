# Payroll — Ubiquitous Language

| Term | Meaning in this context |
|---|---|
| **Worked hours** | Hours a caregiver spent on completed visits in a pay period. |
| **Pay rate** | What the caregiver earns per hour. |
| **Pay period** | Weekly window used to compute a payslip. |

> Payroll is a Generic/Supporting concern here; in a real company this is often a third-party provider behind an Anticorruption Layer.
