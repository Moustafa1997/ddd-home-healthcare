# Scheduling — Ubiquitous Language

| Term | Meaning in this context |
|---|---|
| **Visit** | A scheduled appointment where a caregiver goes to a client's home at a given time to perform tasks. |
| **Caregiver** | A trained staff member who performs visits. |
| **Client** | The person receiving care at home. |
| **Task** | One unit of work inside a visit (e.g. `Medication`, `Meal support`, `Care notes`). |
| **Check-in** | The moment the caregiver arrives at the client's home. |
| **Check-out** | The moment the caregiver leaves. A visit is **completed** only at check-out, and only if the medication task is done. |
| **Visit time** | Start and end of the visit. End must be after start. |

> "Completed" here means *the caregiver left*. It does **not** mean "arrived" and it does **not** mean "paid".
